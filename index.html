Capacitor & Diode Checkers

Red’s turn

Pieces are randomly capacitors or diodes and start with a random orientation (0° / 90° / 180° / 270°).
Diodes may only move diagonally in the direction the triangle is pointing.
Capacitors may move forward or backward (both directions along their axis).
Capacitors can also jump over same-colour pieces; if the jumped piece is a diode it rotates 90° clockwise.
Capture opponents by jumping over them. Reach the opposite end to become a king (then any of the four diagonal directions are allowed).
